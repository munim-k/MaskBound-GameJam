Maskbound (MVP GDD)

Genre
3-Player Co-op Action Combat / Arena-Based

Platform
PC

Target Experience
Fast-paced cooperative combat focused on teamwork, readability, and elemental counterplay. Players must coordinate roles, manage mask corruption, and react to mixed enemy threats in compact arenas. Mastery comes from communication, correct target prioritization, and timely mask swapping.

Core Pillars

Dual Counter System
Player affinity counters enemy family (type).
Masks counter enemy element.

Readable Combat
Enemy silhouettes indicate family.
Enemy VFX and color indicate element.
Damage output clearly reflects effectiveness.

Co-op Dependency
Players rely on each other for optimal damage, revives, and mask swaps.

Arena Pressure
Scripted enemy mixes increase threat through numbers, damage, and health.

Players

Player Count
Exactly 3 players (co-op)

Weapons & Roles

Each player uses a distinct weapon type. Movesets are mechanically similar, but weapon behavior differs.

Sword User
Close-range melee
Wide cleave hitbox
Effective for crowd control at short range

Shoulder Cannon User
Long-range projectile attacks
Single-target focused
Effective for precision damage and flying enemies

Spellcaster
Ranged splash damage
Small area-of-effect on impact
Effective for grouped enemies and zone control

Movement

All players share the same movement abilities:
Walk
Sprint
Jump
Double Jump
Slide

Combat

Simple attacks only (no light/heavy distinction)
No combo trees or advanced inputs

Affinity System

Each player has a permanent affinity that provides passive bonuses (e.g., increased damage or stagger) against one enemy family.

Enemy Families:
Trolls
Scorpion Men
Gargoyles

Affinity is always active and cannot be changed.

Mask System

Masks (3 Total)

Mask
Fire Mask
Countered Element: Ice
Effect: Increased damage vs Ice enemies

Mask
Lightning Mask
Countered Element: Metal
Effect: Increased damage vs Metal enemies

Mask
Earth Mask
Countered Element: Rock
Effect: Increased damage vs Rock enemies

Mask damage buff is active only while worn.
Weapons visually change based on the equipped mask.

Corruption

Wearing a mask continuously builds Corruption.
After 30 seconds, Corruption begins dealing linear damage over time to the wearer.
Corruption damage can knock a player.
Mask timer resets when switching masks.

Corruption Feedback

Mask visually cracks as corruption builds.
Distinct audio warning plays when 5 seconds remain before damage begins.

Mask Swapping

Mask swapping is done via a request/accept UI system.
Successful swaps are instant.
After a swap, both players enter a 5-second mask switch cooldown.
Swapping is blocked while stunned, grabbed, or knocked.

Enemy Design

Enemies are defined by a combination of Family (Type) and Element (Variant).

Enemy Families

Trolls (Brute)
High health
Slow movement
Deal heavy damage
Intended to be handled later in the arena

Scorpion Men (Skirmisher)
Low health
Fast movement
Tail attacks apply poison
Poison deals damage over time
High-pressure harassment enemies

Gargoyles (Control / Flying)
Flying enemies
Swoop attacks
Force vertical awareness and ranged responses

Enemy Elements

Ice
Applies slow
Weak to Fire

Metal
High defense
Weak to Lightning

Rock
High poise
Weak to Earth

Combat Efficiency & Damage Learning

Optimal damage is achieved by combining:
Correct player affinity (family counter)
Correct mask (element counter)

Non-optimal damage is viable but noticeably slower.

Players learn counters organically through:
Higher damage numbers when countering correctly
Faster enemy kills
Stronger hit feedback (visual and audio cues)

No explicit tutorials are required; damage feedback teaches the system.

Arena Structure

Game is arena-based, not wave-based.
Each arena contains scripted enemy spawns.

Difficulty increases via:
Enemy count
Enemy family and element combinations
Increased enemy health and damage

Arena Completion

An arena ends when all enemies are defeated.

Between Arenas

A short downtime area appears before every arena.
A heal station is always available.

Hazards

Simple environmental hazards only.
Spikes deal damage when touched.

Downed & Revive System

Knocked State
Players do not die immediately.
When health reaches zero, players are knocked.

Reviving
Any single player can revive a knocked teammate.
Revive is timed: 10 seconds.
Revives can be interrupted by enemy damage.
Revived players return with low health.

Failure Condition
Game over occurs when all players are knocked simultaneously.

Win Condition

Successfully clear all arenas in the demo.

Scope & Intent

Jam-only MVP
No progression, unlocks, or meta systems
No long-term player upgrades

Focused on demonstrating:
Dual counter combat
Mask swapping tension
Co-op coordination

Design Summary

Maskbound is a 3-player cooperative arena combat game built around elemental masks, role identity, and readable enemy design. The MVP emphasizes moment-to-moment teamwork, risk management through corruption, and learning through combat feedback rather than tutorials. The experience is tightly scoped to deliver a clear and replayable demo.
