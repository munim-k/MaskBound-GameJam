Maskbound

Genre: 3-Player Co-op Action Combat / Wave-Based Arena
Platform: PC

Target Experience
Fast-paced co-op combat focused on maximizing team performance through player roles (affinity) and mask-based elemental counters. Mastery requires communication and active mask swapping.

Core Pillars
Dual Counter System: Player affinity counters enemy type; masks counter enemy element.
Readable Combat: Enemy silhouette indicates type; VFX/color indicates element.
Co-op Dependency: Coordination is essential for success.
Wave Escalation: Increasing pressure and enemy complexity per wave.

Player & Affinity
Three co-op players, each with a natural affinity providing passive bonuses (damage, stagger) against one of three enemy families: Trolls, Scorpion Men, Gargoyles. Affinity is always active.

Masks System (3 Total)
Masks must be rotated to prevent Corruption. After 30 seconds of continuous use, a mask deals damage-over-time to the wearer, forcing a swap. Swapping resets the timer.

Mask List
Fire Mask
Countered Element: Ice
Utility: Burn, cleanse slow

Lightning Mask
Countered Element: Metal
Utility: Shock, armor break

Earth Mask
Countered Element: Rock
Utility: Shatter, stagger

Enemies
Enemies combine one of three Families (Types) with one of three Elements (Variants).

Types
Trolls – Brute
Scorpion Men – Skirmisher
Gargoyles – Control

Elements
Ice – Slow, weak to Fire
Metal – Defense, weak to Lightning
Rock – Poise, weak to Earth

Combat System
Efficiency is determined by the Dual Counter System:
Correct Player Affinity + Correct Mask = Optimal Efficiency.
Non-optimal combat is viable but slower and riskier.

Mask Swapping – Request Swap System
To maintain combat flow and consent, swapping is done via a request/accept UI system.

Player A requests a mask from Player B.
Player B accepts or declines instantly.
Successful swap is instant but initiates a 3–5 second cooldown for both.
Swaps require explicit acceptance and are blocked during stun, grab, or downed states.

Gameplay Loop
Identify enemy types and elements.
Communicate and coordinate roles and mask strategy.
Swap masks as necessary due to enemy threats or Corruption.
Engage and survive the wave.
Recover, prepare, repeat.

Failure: All players downed.
Success: Clear all waves or reach score threshold.

Design Summary
Maskbound is a wave-based co-op combat game defined by role identity and elemental mask strategy. Mastery requires synergizing player affinity and mask element against mixed enemy threats.
